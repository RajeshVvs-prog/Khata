03 — What's on every page

Six habits of a good ledger.

01

Reads plain language, not forms

Type "spent 450 on dinner yesterday" and Khata files it as ₹450, Food, yesterday, expense — no dropdowns.

02

Catches the entry that doesn't belong

It learns your normal shopping range, then tells you the moment a line is 200%+ off it — like the Reliance Digital entry above.

03

Predicts next month's page

A simple regression over your history writes a forecast row for every category before the month even starts.

04

Finds the subscriptions you forgot

Netflix, Spotify and Prime get grouped into one recurring total, with a nudge when one goes unused.

05

Tracks a goal like a running balance

Set "Buy a bike, ₹1,20,000" and watch the remaining balance count down with every saved rupee.

06

Makes discipline visible

Streaks and small badges for the boring wins — a no-spend week counts for more than it feels like.
